This course presents techniques for the discovery of patterns hidden in large data sets, focusing on issues relating to their feasibility, usefulness, effectiveness, and scalability. As a result, the course is a comprehensive introduction to data mining, presented with effectiveness and scalability issues in focus. It should be useful for computing science students, application developers, and business professionals at master degree, as well as researchers involved in any of the disciplines listed above.</td></tr>
