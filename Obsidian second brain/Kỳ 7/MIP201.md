<tr><td><b>Description:</b></td><td>Hands-on Embedded Systems Development: Learn to implement real-world embedded system projects on microcontrollers (using both simulation environments and physical hardware) with the STM32Cube HAL library and STM32CubeIDE.<br>Peripheral Mastery &amp; ARM Architecture: Master the core architecture of the ARM Cortex-M processor. Develop and refine C/C++ source code to build diverse applications (signal generators, temperature monitoring systems, audio players, and user interfaces) by leveraging Digital/Analog I/O, Interrupts, Timers, and Pulse-Width Modulation (PWM). Gain hands-on proficiency in analyzing and deploying serial communication protocols—SPI, UART, and I2C—while evaluating their advantages and limitations in embedded environments.<br>Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS): Explore the core characteristics of RTOS and multitasking mechanisms on microcontrollers. Students will directly apply Threads, Mutexes, Semaphores, and Interrupts to design a complete multitasking system, effectively resolving resource contention issues.<br>Practical Engineering Skills: Prototype embedded systems using the ST Nucleo-F401RE (ST Nucleo-F411RE) development board and cost-effective components. Enhance hardware-level C/C++ programming mindsets by immediately applying theory to practice. Master the end-to-end hardware development workflow alongside professional debugging and troubleshooting techniques.</td></tr>

<tr><td><b>Tools:</b></td><td>Specific Tools:<br>1. Hardware &amp; Training Kits<br>- Development Board: ST Nucleo-F401RE (powered by the ARM Cortex-M4 core).<br>- Programmer &amp; Debugger: On-board ST-Link/V2 debugger, supporting the SWD (Serial Wire Debug) protocol.<br>2. Software Development Environment<br>- Primary IDE: STM32CubeIDE (fully replaces Keil Studio Cloud and Mbed Studio). This is the industry-standard IDE for C/C++ development on ST microcontrollers.<br>- Simulation Software: Wokwi (a free, web-based open-source simulator supporting STM32 boards and various peripherals) or other debugging/simulation tools recommended by the instructor.</td></tr>
