<tr><td><b>Description:</b></td><td>The Graduation Thesis Startup Project is the third step of experience that brings together the startup project  developed in EXE101 and EXE201 in a further perspective. It enables students to continously enhance their whole project&#x27;s performannce under the guidance of mentor, instructor and other supports of the university. <br>Beside the startup project, students will demonstrate, according to a guide reserved for them, the contribution of their major&#x27;s knowledge and skills in various components of the project. Students will complete writing of the final business plan in a formal format and defending their arguments in front of the examination board.<br>The examination will be given in such areas as (1) project background (2) market analysis, (3) value proposition (4) technology touch (5) major&#x27;s requirement (6) key activity plan, (7) financial analysis, (8) future roadmap.</td></tr>
