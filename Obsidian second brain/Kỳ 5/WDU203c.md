Students will gain hands-on experience with taking a product from initial concept, through user research, ideation and refinement, formal analysis, prototyping, and user testing, applying perspectives and methods to ensure a great user experience at every step. This course concludes with a capstone project, in which learners will incorporate UX Research and Design principles to design a complete product, taking it from an initial concept to an interactive prototype.</td></tr>
